Before you join

Do I need experience?

No prior movement experience is needed for the introductory formats. Each invitation offers options for participation, including observing and resting.

What about accessibility?

Ask about venue access, sensory conditions and seated participation before booking. Final access information will accompany each confirmed event.

How does booking work?

This preview demonstrates the enquiry journey. Confirmed dates, prices, cancellation terms and a booking provider will be added before public launch.
