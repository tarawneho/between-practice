What happens in the room

Simple movement prompts, pauses and collective exercises invite attention to the body and the room around it. There is no choreography to master. Participants can move, sit, observe or rest. The format can also be commissioned for a group.

Care & participation

Participation is an invitation. Consent, personal boundaries and the option to pause are part of the format. We discuss access needs in advance and make the conditions of each session clear.
