05 / About the practice

A studio for work
between disciplines.

Between Practice is a temporary name for an independent production practice working across embodiment, performance, artistic research and collective learning.

A form that can grow
with the work.

The practice brings projects, programs and collaborations together without asking them to fit one discipline. Producing is the connecting thread: creating the conditions for ideas to become shared experiences.

Work can take the form of a performance, a class, a research project, a community gathering or individual support. Collaborators join according to the needs of each project.

The artist’s approved biography, name, location and collaborator credits will be added here. The structure leaves room for a wider team and future editions.
