Film / queer joy

POOLBOY

A celebration of queer joy, connection and playfulness, set against the backdrop of a shimmering blue pool.

All work ↗

Laughter, swimming and shared moments bring diverse identities together. Water becomes a metaphor for fluidity: a space where boundaries dissolve and individual stories interweave.

Shot on a handheld camera phone, the film was filmed, edited and first screened during an artist residency in the German countryside.
