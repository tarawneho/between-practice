Performance / collaboration

(Embodied) tools for decentralization

A sound tool turns the performers’ movements into a shared exploration of bodily, social and spatial orientation.

All work ↗

Integrated into costume sleeves, mobile devices produce melodic digital sounds. Movement modulates the sounds in real time; the performers respond to the evolving soundscape in a situation of mutual affectivity.

The sound-generator prototype uses sensors in conventional smartphones and was developed with artist and computer scientist Jonathan Schneider. The performance draws on decentralised movement patterns of migratory birds and schools of fish.

Presented within FABULATIVE LORE: Tools and Handiness, the work explores how objects carry stories, scores and possibilities for interaction.
