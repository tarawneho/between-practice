Performance / collective practice

Time Smuggler

The body as architecture. A collaborative performance exploring how bodies in relation transform our experience of space and time.

All work ↗

Initiated by Isabel Lewis and developed with artists from the Class for Performative Arts at the Academy of Fine Arts Leipzig, Time Smuggler creates temporary architectures within existing architecture. Bodies, movement, gestures and voices extend spatial structures and create shifting compositions.

Dreaming and dérive inform the approach. Drawing on Alina Popa’s Imitation of Dream and Guy Debord’s Theory of the Dérive, the ensemble shifts habitual patterns of movement and perception.

An evolving vocabulary of scores provides starting points for improvisation. Each iteration responds to the architecture, rhythms and social conditions of its surroundings.

Editions & contexts

  • Ringlokschuppen, Leipzig
  • Galerie für Zeitgenössische Kunst, Leipzig — adapted for The Word for Museum is Forest
  • Ocean Space and public space, Venice — October 2024
TIME SMUGGLER proposed a collective art-making process, one that incorporates individual positions in ways that empower their voices and textures, rather than compromising them.

Eleni Michaelidi · curator and art historian · curatorial statement, December 2023
