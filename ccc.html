CCC — Cash Character Courage

An interactive, spontaneous and participatory lap-dance / striptease performance. A work that changes with its venue, audience and moment.

All work ↗

From the supplied interview draft

When does a performance become a piece?

“This piece shifts sight and audience accordingly. Its power lies in its ephemera, the constellation of the moment.”

The artist’s draft reflects on a piece emerging through different contexts: the strip club, a community space and a theatre stage. A change in venue and audience also changes interaction, recognition and how the performer understands the work.

What changes with the audience?

The interview traces the difference between a booked performance and an encounter that happens spontaneously. It asks how the same work can be read differently across artistic and working contexts, and how those boundaries might become more fluid.
