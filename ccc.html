Bookable performance / interview

CCC — Cash Character Courage

Lapdance$Striptease Performance (interactive / spontaneous / participatory)

All work ↗

English

Interview / Text

This piece shifts sight and audience accordingly. Its power lies in its ephemera, the constellation of the moment. I didn’t actually know I was working on a piece until I was booked by Alias and Roy Dick to perform at their Dragshow in a theatre. I didn’t know a piece had been emerging in different states of rhizomatic metamorphosis. Of course, first I performed CCC, it did not have a title then, in the strip club. Whilst it was a performance, sure, I didn’t consider it a work of art. It transitioned to feeling like art or a piece or a performance piece, a stage piece – urgh this is confusing – when venue and audience changes. What changed here was audience reaction and interaction. Also that once the piece was over I was interacted with as the artist, a friend, as community member etc. I am not sure as what I was interacted in the strip club; yes also different things but you are, or I felt more replaceable there.

The first time I was booked to perform in a BI_PoC arts and community space was, it uplifted my heart. Ah yes, my language seems to distinguish between booked performance and just performance. It is terminology still evolving. But what I mean, well. There is a difference in how I perceive myself depending on circumstances. Prestige maybe? There is a difference in performing and busking in the streets as opposed to a stage at a festival or theatre let’s say. I would like to make these feelings more fluid, less domainbound? These days I do feel better getting paid as an artist then getting paid as a stripper. For the same work.

Which pays better? [laughs] You’re probably talking about hard currency here. These days the hoe gets paid better than the artist.
