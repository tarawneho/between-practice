Artistic research / cartography

PHANTASMAGORIA — Kartographische Morphismen

Cartographic material becomes abstract form: a way of travelling through places, news, memory and feeling.

All work ↗

Places and coordinates become symbols for experiences, stories and events that leave a deep impression. Abstract two-dimensional objects made from cartographic material respond to places that briefly enter the centre of attention before disappearing into the periphery.

The works record journeys that are mental and emotional rather than physical. Maps, a portal to imagination and learning since childhood, form the visual starting point.

The process began during the Covid-19 lockdowns and continues as an ongoing practice of reflection and processing.
