What happens in the room

We begin with a question from your group. Through movement, listening and structured conversation, we create a format that supports exchange without demanding that everyone participate in the same way.

Care & participation

Participation is an invitation. Consent, personal boundaries and the option to pause are part of the format. We discuss access needs in advance and make the conditions of each session clear.
